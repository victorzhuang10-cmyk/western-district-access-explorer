FIELDNOTES / METHOD

Access, measured
by the walk.

The 100 m–2 km slider sets a walking-time budget using a 1 m/s reference pace (for example, 300 m = 5 minutes). Places and the mapped reach are included by route travel time, then screened by the steepest sampled route slope. Google Maps Routes time is used when its backend is configured; the public static preview uses Lands Department 3D Pedestrian Route times.

The shaded reach is a sampled contour, not a complete isochrone. Google walking routes can omit clear sidewalks or pedestrian paths; neither route source verifies steps, kerbs, surface quality, crossing safety, step-free access, equipment condition, or opening hours. Public open-space records are points, not green parcel boundaries. Field checks are needed before product decisions.
