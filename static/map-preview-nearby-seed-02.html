FIELDNOTES / METHOD

Access, measured
by the walk.

The 100 m–2 km slider is a walking-time budget at a 1 m/s reference pace (for example, 300 m = 5 minutes). There is no official buffer polygon in the map data. The app estimates one from 24 Lands Department 3D pedestrian routes toward targets at 15° intervals, cutting each route at its time or grade limit and joining the reached endpoints.

The displayed buffer expands by unioning the whole OpenFreeMap building footprints touched by the sampled boundary. In 2D, its fill sits beneath building footprints; in 3D Reach’s carved diagram, the buffer and edge remain visible and all visible buildings are rendered once, with buildings intersecting reach jade and the rest white. A touched footprint is only a candidate: this does not confirm that an entrance connects to the reachable path or that the building is publicly accessible. Destination routes are still calculated separately, and the 24-direction boundary remains approximate.
