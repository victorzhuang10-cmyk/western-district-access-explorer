FIELDNOTES / METHOD

Access, measured
by the walk.

The 100 m–2 km slider is a walking-time budget at a 1 m/s reference pace (for example, 300 m = 5 minutes). The filled buffer follows sampled pedestrian-network routes; it is not a circle. Route acceptance uses both the overall start-to-end grade and the steepest grade between consecutive 3D elevation samples.

The public preview uses the Lands Department 3D Pedestrian Route service and does not require Google Maps billing. Its buffer is estimated from 24 route directions; unsampled or blocked directions remain gaps. Open-space records are points rather than park parcel boundaries, so the shaded area clips locations, not whole parks. Steps, kerbs, surfaces, crossing safety, step-free entrances, equipment condition, and opening hours need field checks.
