FIELDNOTES / METHOD

Access, measured
by the walk.

The map loads Hong Kong CSDI point records for public open spaces and LCSD outdoor fitness facilities around the study origin. Walking routes, lengths and elevation samples come from the Lands Department 3D Pedestrian Route service. Google Routes and Photorealistic 3D Tiles can be enabled on a separately configured backend.

The threshold is route distance (100 m–2 km), and slope is the steepest elevation change between returned 3D samples. This does not verify steps, kerbs, surface quality, crossing safety, step-free access, equipment condition, or opening hours. Public open-space points are entrance/location records, not green parcel boundaries; field verification is needed before product decisions.
