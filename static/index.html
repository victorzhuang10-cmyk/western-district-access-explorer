FIELDNOTES / METHOD

Access, measured
by the walk.

This prototype compares walking routes from an approximate community-centre origin with nearby public green spaces and outdoor fitness points. Google route distances and sampled elevation are used when configured.

Grade is estimated between elevation samples. It cannot detect steps, kerbs, surface quality, crossing safety, or whether a facility is open. The points and routes currently visible are illustrative demo data and must be replaced with verified GeoInfo / CSDI records and checked in the field.
